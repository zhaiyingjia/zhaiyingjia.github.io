Yingjia Zhai

Assistant Professor of Economics · School of Economics

I study how technological change, public policies, and spatial economic interactions shape firm behavior, innovation, and labor market outcomes.

My research draws on large-scale microeconomic data, including firm-level, patent, and mobility data, to examine urban and regional development, technological innovation, and public policy.
